# SHIFT Chapter 3 / Level 41–60 — candidate QA and Perfect Shift contract

**Status:** Not release-ready. Draft LevelData assets are separate from the main 40-level campaign and MORA remains frozen.

## The 51–60 difficulty plan

| Level range | Shortest winning route (independent model) | Longer, valid winning route | Move limit | Desired reasoning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 51–54 | 7 | 8 | 9 | Alternative orders and state preparation |
| 55–56 | 8 | 9 | 10 | Two-color routes, chained preparation |
| 57–59 | 9 | 10 | 11 | Misleading shortcuts, delayed responses |
| 60 | 10 | 11 | 12 | Finale combining two colors and gates |

Both paths for every candidate are stored in the source branch: the shortest in LevelData.knownSolution, the alternate in Chapter3AlternateSolutions.cs. Do not ship the second path as a hint. A valid longer solution is a real win, not a failure.

**Perfect Shift design:** awarded only when accepted, committed taps equal the **certified absolute minimum**. KnownSolution is *not* proof: the authoritative Unity solver must prove the minimum and bind it to the exact board fingerprint before the production registry is updated. Do not create speculative Perfect certificates. Current VerifiedOptimality.Get() returns null for 41–60, so the new draft levels cannot earn Perfect yet; this is an intentional release gate.

## Verification order
1. Unity 6.3 LTS: SHIFT > Chapter 3 > Validate Draft Levels 41-60. Logs 20 candidates, completion and certified minimum.
2. Editor tests: Chapter3DraftTests (41–50), Chapter3AlternativeRouteTests (51–60). The second test replays two distinct paths using BoardManager and fails on unresolved search bound.
3. SHIFT > Chapter 3 > Create Isolated Preview Scene, then select Level 41–60 using Preview Controls in Play Mode. Original Prototype.unity and player saves remain unchanged.
4. Human design pass: compare player confusion and moment of realization; measure completion time, failed/restarted levels, hint usage, reactive satisfaction. Replace repetitive boards or cosmetic reskins rather than pretending they are finished.
5. Release later: chapter 3 UI and localization, help/hints, save migration, chapter ending, VerifiedOptimality fingerprint certificates, MORA actual completion milestones. Do not merge to main before QA.

### Scope
The 51–60 boards were generated by hand-directed changes and recombinations of existing SHIFT mechanics, then filtered via a standalone behavioural model matching BoardManager.cs. Independent model replay checks both routes, with minimum lengths from 7 to 10, but cannot replace Unity Editor certification. Consequently **design candidates, not final handcrafted chapters**.

Main branch, first 40 level assets, existing MORA branch, player progression, ad pipeline and published app are untouched.
